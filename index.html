About Me

I'm Naume Murungi, a Web Developer and Designer who loves building websites and creating digital experiences. My journey started with curiosity about how the websites I used every day actually worked, and that curiosity grew into a real passion for turning ideas into clean, functional, well-designed products on the web.

My mission is to build digital experiences that are simple, useful, and genuinely enjoyable to use — solving real problems for the people on the other side of the screen, not just making things that look good.
